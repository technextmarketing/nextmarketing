🏢 Client Overview

LearnEdge Academy is an online professional education platform offering short-course certifications in project management, data analytics, and business finance — priced between RM490 and RM1,200 per course. The platform serves working professionals in Malaysia looking to upskill without committing to a full degree programme.

The business was running Google Ads at RM6,800/month and achieving a steady 4,200 landing page visits per month. However, enrolment conversion rate sat at 1.9% — meaning 98.1% of paid traffic was leaving without purchasing. At 1.9%, the cost per enrolment was RM850. The target was RM300.

LearnEdge's marketing team suspected the landing page was the bottleneck — but had no data to confirm where users were dropping off or why. The CRO Audit engagement was commissioned to diagnose the problem, prioritise fixes, and validate improvements through A/B testing.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • Conversion rate of 1.9% on a page receiving 4,200 paid visits/month — costing RM850 per enrolment against a course price of RM490–RM1,200
  • No heatmap, session recording, or scroll depth data — all CRO decisions had been based on intuition rather than user behaviour evidence
  • Hero section headline was generic: "Advance Your Career with Online Certifications" — no mention of course names, delivery format, duration, or outcomes
  • Enrolment CTA button ("Enrol Now — RM490") appeared only at the bottom of the page — 74% of visitors never scrolled that far, according to Clarity data collected post-installation
  • Social proof section had 3 testimonials with stock photo avatars — immediately recognisable as generic and undermining trust in a high-intent purchase decision
  • Course details section used a small accordion with 8pt font — on mobile (62% of traffic), all course information was hidden behind an extra tap that most users never discovered
  • Page load time on mobile: 5.8 seconds — every second above 3 seconds reduces conversion by 7% (Google benchmark). LearnEdge was losing an estimated 20% of mobile users before the page fully loaded
📋 Our Approach — 60 Days
Week 1–2
Clarity Installation & Data Collection
  • Installed Microsoft Clarity tracking script on the landing page — zero development cost, free tool. Collected 14 days of heatmap and session recording data before touching a single element on the page
  • Analysed scroll depth: only 26% of visitors scrolled past the testimonials section; only 12% reached the CTA button — meaning the primary conversion element was invisible to 88% of traffic
  • Watched 28 session recordings: identified rage click pattern on the course module accordion (users tapping repeatedly because it wasn't visually obvious as interactive on mobile)
  • Documented 7 priority issues in Google Docs with Clarity screenshots: each given a severity rating (High/Medium/Low), fix recommendation, and estimated conversion impact
Week 3–5
Implementation — 7 UX Fixes
  • Fix 1 (Critical): Moved enrolment CTA button from bottom to directly below hero section — above the fold on both desktop and mobile. Immediate 3.2% uplift on first test day
  • Fix 2 (Critical): Rewrote hero headline using Claude AI — new version: "Get Certified in Data Analytics in 8 Weeks — Study Online, Weekends Only" — specific, outcome-driven, and time-framed
  • Fix 3 (High): Replaced 3 stock photo testimonials with real student photos provided by LearnEdge (4 students agreed to be featured) + LinkedIn profile links for verification
  • Fix 4 (High): Replaced accordion course details on mobile with an always-visible tabbed layout — course information now visible without any extra interaction required
  • Fix 5 (Medium): Added urgency element: "Next cohort starts 1 May 2026 — 8 spots remaining" — dynamic counter updated weekly by the LearnEdge team
  • Fix 6 (Medium): Compressed all images on the page using TinyPNG — mobile load time reduced from 5.8 to 2.4 seconds
  • Fix 7 (Medium): Added a sticky bottom CTA bar on mobile: always-visible "Enrol Now" button regardless of scroll position — eliminated the issue of users losing the CTA as they scroll
Week 6–8
A/B Testing & Result Validation
  • Ran headline A/B test: original vs new Claude AI headline — alternated landing page URL weekly, tracked GA4 conversions separately. New headline: 4.1% conversion rate vs original 1.9% — 116% lift from headline change alone
  • Ran CTA button colour test: original blue (#0A2540) vs high-contrast amber (#D97706) — amber version achieved 18% higher click rate in GA4 event tracking
  • Final conversion rate with all 7 fixes and winning A/B variants active: 5.8% — a 205% improvement over the baseline 1.9%
  • Cost per enrolment dropped from RM850 to RM279 — below the RM300 target. The same RM6,800/month ad budget now generates 24 enrolments vs the previous 8
  • Delivered 60-day CRO audit report: Clarity screenshots, session recording insights, A/B test results, fix implementation log, and 5 next-priority experiments for Month 3
