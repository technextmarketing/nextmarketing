🏢 Client Overview

ProSkill Academy offers professional certification programmes in project management, finance, and leadership for working adults in Klang Valley. With over a decade in the training industry, they had accumulated a contact list of 4,200 email addresses — former students, enquiries, and event attendees.

Despite this asset sitting in their CRM, the last email campaign sent to the list was 18 months prior. The contacts had gone cold, and the marketing team (2 part-time staff) lacked the tools and templates to restart email marketing effectively.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • 4,200 contacts with no email touchpoint in 18 months — high risk of spam flags on restart
  • Previous open rate was 8% — well below the education industry benchmark of 28%
  • No email template — campaigns were plain text with no visual branding
  • No segmentation — all contacts treated identically regardless of course interest or stage
  • No automation — every email was manually composed and sent from a personal Gmail account
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1 · Jan
Setup — Platform, Template & Re-Engagement
  • Imported 4,200 contacts into Brevo — cleaned list: removed 187 hard bounces, 92 duplicates → clean list: 3,921
  • Built branded email template in Canva (600×200 header) + Brevo drag-and-drop: header / 2-column body / CTA button / social footer
  • Campaign 1 (re-engagement): Subject A/B test — "We miss you, [First Name]" vs. "Your ProSkill access is still waiting" — sent to 3,921 contacts
  • Used Brevo's 7-day re-engagement window — contacts who didn't open were suppressed from next campaign to protect sender score
  • Set up welcome automation sequence: 3-email drip for any new opt-ins from website form
Month 2 · Feb
Revenue — Course Promotion Campaign
  • Segmented re-engaged contacts (those who opened Month 1) into "warm" list: 2,840 contacts
  • Campaign 2: PMP Certification intake promotion with early-bird pricing — deadline-driven CTA ("Offer ends Friday")
  • Subject line winner from A/B test: "Get PMP certified in 3 weekends — early-bird closes Friday" (41% open rate)
  • Campaign generated 24 enrolment enquiries → 18 paid registrations at RM400 = RM7,200 attributed revenue
  • Campaign 3 (newsletter): industry news + student spotlight — maintained relationship between promotions
Month 3 · Mar
Scale — Second Revenue Campaign + Click Optimisation
  • Campaign 4: Financial Modelling course promotion — targeting finance segment (tagged from contact import data)
  • Applied learnings from Feb: deadline CTA, single focused CTA button, personalised subject line
  • 27 enquiries → 27 paid registrations at RM400 = RM10,800 attributed revenue
  • Click rate rose to 12% — above education benchmark of 5–7%
  • Delivered email performance dashboard in Google Sheets: open rate, CTR, unsubs, revenue per campaign
