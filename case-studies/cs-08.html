🏢 Client Overview

ProSkill Academy offers professional certification programmes in project management, finance, and leadership for working adults in Klang Valley. With over a decade in the training industry, they had accumulated a contact list of 4,200 email addresses — former students, enquiries, and event attendees.

Despite this asset sitting in their CRM, the last email campaign sent to the list was 18 months prior. The contacts had gone cold, and the marketing team (2 part-time staff) lacked the tools and templates to restart email marketing effectively.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • 4,200 contacts with no email touchpoint in 18 months — high risk of spam flags on restart
  • Previous open rate was 8% — well below the education industry benchmark of 28%
  • No email template — campaigns were plain text with no visual branding
  • No segmentation — all contacts treated identically regardless of course interest or stage
  • No automation — every email was manually composed and sent from a personal Gmail account
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1 · Jan
Setup — Platform, Infrastructure & Re-Engagement
  • Migrated the full 4,200-contact list to a professional email platform — cleaning to 3,921 active contacts by removing bounces and duplicates, establishing a clean foundation before any sends were made.
  • Built a fully branded email template and launched the first campaign to the list in 18 months — an A/B-tested re-engagement send designed to identify warm contacts while protecting sender reputation for future campaigns.
  • Set up a 3-email welcome automation for all new opt-ins — ensuring every future subscriber received a structured onboarding journey from their first touchpoint with ProSkill Academy.
Month 2 · Feb
Revenue — First Course Promotion Campaign
  • Segmented the 2,840 re-engaged contacts from Month 1 and launched the first promotional campaign — a PMP Certification intake with early-bird pricing and a deadline-driven call to action, achieving a 41% open rate.
  • The PMP campaign generated 24 enrolment enquiries, converting 18 into paid registrations at RM400 each — RM7,200 in directly attributed revenue from a single email send.
  • Sent a relationship-building newsletter between promotions — maintaining list warmth and establishing a sustainable sending cadence that kept the audience engaged without fatigue.
Month 3 · Mar
Scale — Second Revenue Campaign & Performance Results
  • Launched the second revenue campaign — a Financial Modelling course promotion targeted to the finance interest segment, applying all learnings from February to sharpen the copy, CTA, and delivery timing.
  • 27 enquiries converted to 27 paid registrations — RM10,800 in attributed revenue. Click rate reached 12%, more than double the education industry benchmark of 5–7%.
  • Delivered a full email performance dashboard tracking open rates, CTR, unsubscribes, and revenue per campaign — giving the ProSkill team complete visibility for independent management going forward.
