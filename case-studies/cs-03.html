🏢 Client Overview

Makan House is a casual dining brand with three outlets in Petaling Jaya, Bangsar, and Cheras. Known locally for its affordable Malaysian fusion menu, the brand had built its customer base entirely through word-of-mouth and food delivery platforms over five years of operation.

Despite consistent foot traffic during peak hours, the business had no structured social media presence. Posts were occasional and inconsistent in style — often just phone photos with no caption strategy, no brand identity, and no content schedule across any of the three locations.

⚠️ The Challenge

When we audited the accounts in December 2025, the situation was clear:

  • Instagram had 890 followers — and was losing ~28 followers per month due to extended inactivity
  • Average post reach was 3,400 — almost entirely existing followers, zero new account discovery
  • The last Facebook post was 6 weeks prior; last Reel posted was 4 months prior
  • Each outlet posted with different logos, fonts, and colours — no unified visual identity
  • Engagement rate sat at 1.2% — F&B industry benchmark is 3.5% or higher
  • Only 4 walk-in customers per month cited social media as their discovery channel
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1 · Jan
Foundation — Brand Identity & Content Launch
  • Unified the brand identity across all three outlets for the first time — establishing a consistent visual system that made Makan House immediately recognisable across both platforms.
  • Launched the first structured 8-post content calendar covering food showcases, behind-the-scenes moments, promotions, and customer features — each format targeting a specific audience intent, published consistently on a twice-weekly schedule.
  • Published all 8 posts on schedule and cleared all 23 unanswered legacy comments within 24 hours — reactivating the brand's presence and sending strong positive engagement signals to both audiences and the algorithm.
Month 2 · Feb
Engagement — Community Growth & Format Expansion
  • Expanded content formats to include Instagram Stories and a recurring 'Customer of the Week' series — deepening audience connection and turning regulars into organic content contributors.
  • Weekly performance data revealed food showcase posts outperforming promotions 2.8% vs. 1.9% on engagement — this insight directly drove the Month 3 rebalance toward 5 food showcases and fewer promotional posts.
  • Managed 72 community interactions across post comments and DMs — consistent responsiveness built audience trust and maintained the brand's visibility in the algorithm's reach pool throughout the month.
Month 3 · Mar
Scale — Reach Milestone & Lead Attribution
  • Added "Show this post for 10% off" CTA to 2 promotional posts — directly attributed to 11 tracked walk-ins via POS code
  • Produced first branded Reel: 30-second kitchen behind-the-scenes — reached 6,200 accounts (highest single-post reach of the engagement)
  • Engagement rate peaked at 4.8% in Week 3 — above F&B benchmark of 3.5%
  • Delivered Month 3 analytics report: reach per post, engagement trend, top content breakdown, follower growth, walk-in attribution
  • Handed over Month 4 content calendar with performance-based content mix applied
