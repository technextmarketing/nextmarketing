🏢 Client Overview

PearlDent Dental Group operates three dental clinics in Petaling Jaya: SS2, Ara Damansara, and Bandar Utama. The group sees approximately 840 patient visits per month across all three locations and has been operating for 8 years. Services range from general dentistry and orthodontics to cosmetic procedures including veneers and teeth whitening.

Despite 8 years of operation and a loyal patient base, PearlDent had accumulated only 43 Google reviews in total across all three locations. More critically, the Bandar Utama location had a 3.2-star rating due to two unanswered negative reviews from 2024 — both citing long waiting times — that sat publicly visible with no clinic response. The SS2 location had a 4.4-star rating; Ara Damansara had 4.2. Average across three locations: 3.9 stars.

When new patients in Petaling Jaya searched "dentist near me" or "dental clinic SS2", PearlDent was appearing in results but losing click-throughs to competitor clinics with 4.8+ ratings and 150+ reviews. The Reputation Management engagement focused on closing this gap through review volume growth, consistent response management, and proactive monitoring.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • Only 43 Google reviews after 8 years — an average of 5.3 reviews per year, suggesting the clinic had never actively requested reviews from its satisfied patient base
  • Bandar Utama location sitting at 3.2 stars due to 2 unanswered negative reviews — publicly visible for over 12 months with no clinic response, compounding the reputational damage
  • No monitoring system: staff were unaware of new reviews unless they happened to check the Google Business Profile dashboard — which happened rarely
  • No review solicitation process: happy patients left the clinic without any prompt to share their experience. The few reviews that existed were from patients who independently decided to leave them
  • All three Google Business Profiles (SS2, Ara Damansara, Bandar Utama) had incomplete information: missing service lists, outdated operating hours (two locations showed COVID-era reduced hours still active), and no photos updated since 2022
  • No staff awareness of online reputation: reception teams did not know the clinic's current Google rating, had never been asked to encourage reviews, and had no script or tool for doing so
  • Click-through rate from Google Search: PearlDent appearing in search results but estimated 40% click-through deficit vs competitors with 4.7+ ratings — patients choosing competitor clinics based on rating before even visiting the PearlDent website
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1
Audit, Crisis Response & Google Profile Fixes
  • Set up Google Alerts: alerts for "PearlDent", "Pearl Dent", "PearlDent dental", and each location name — "As it happens" delivery to clinic Gmail. First alert fired within 4 hours: a new 2-star review had been left at Bandar Utama overnight
  • Exported all 43 existing reviews to Google Sheets: Rating / Date / Text / Platform / Response status. Found: 18 reviews with no response (including the 2 damaging Bandar Utama reviews from 2024)
  • Used Claude AI to draft professional responses to all 18 unanswered reviews: negative reviews acknowledged the issue sincerely, offered to resolve offline, and included direct contact email; positive reviews personalised by service mentioned
  • Published responses to all 18 reviews within 72 hours — including the 2 long-standing Bandar Utama negatives. Both reviewers were also contacted via the email address they had registered with the clinic. One updated their review from 1-star to 3-star after the clinic followed up personally
  • Completed all 3 Google Business Profiles: updated hours, added 24 new clinic photos (waiting area, treatment rooms, team), wrote service descriptions for 8 core treatments using Claude AI, added FAQ section to each profile
Month 2
Proactive Review Solicitation System
  • Wrote review request message using Claude AI: "Hi [Name], thank you for visiting PearlDent [Location] today. If you had a positive experience, we'd really appreciate a Google review — it takes about 30 seconds and helps other patients find us. [Direct Google review link]. Thank you! — Dr [Name]'s team"
  • Implemented review request via Brevo WhatsApp automation: trigger = appointment marked "completed" in clinic booking system → 24-hour delay → WhatsApp message sent automatically with direct Google review link
  • Manual fallback: receptionist SMS script for patients without WhatsApp — sent via clinic phone after each completed appointment session
  • Month 2 results: 28 new reviews received (vs historical average of 5–6 per month). All 28 responded to within 24 hours using Claude AI-drafted replies reviewed by the clinic manager
  • Google ratings by Month 2 end: SS2 (4.4 → 4.6), Ara Damansara (4.2 → 4.6), Bandar Utama (3.2 → 3.9) — all moving positively as fresh 5-star reviews diluted older negatives
Month 3
Volume Scaling & Reputation Maintenance System
  • Added review request to post-treatment follow-up: patients who had orthodontic or cosmetic procedures (highest satisfaction cases) received a personalised WhatsApp from the treating dentist directly — not just from "the clinic" — achieving 38% review completion rate vs 22% from generic clinic messages
  • Month 3 reviews received: 37 new reviews — 33 five-star, 3 four-star, 1 three-star. All responded to within 24 hours
  • Total reviews at end of Month 3: 43 (baseline) + 28 (Month 2) + 37 (Month 3) = 108 reviews — more than doubling the total review count in 60 days
  • Google ratings at Month 3 end: SS2 (4.8), Ara Damansara (4.7), Bandar Utama (4.6). Weighted average across 3 locations: 4.7 stars
  • Delivered reputation management report + permanent SOP: review request messaging templates, response SOP (4 tone variants for positive/negative reviews), weekly Google Sheets tracking sheet, and Google Alerts configuration guide
