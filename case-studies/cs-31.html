Client Overview

PaySwift Fintech is a Malaysian B2B payment automation platform helping SMEs streamline accounts payable, supplier payments, and multi-currency transactions. After securing a seed round in late 2025, the company needed to build a scalable sales function from scratch — moving from founder-led sales that closed 3–4 deals per quarter to a repeatable revenue engine with a dedicated full-time rep.

The company had a defined ICP (finance managers and CFOs at SMEs with 20–200 employees) but no documented sales process, no CRM, and no onboarding kit for a new hire. The founding CEO had been closing all deals personally — often spending 40% of her time in sales conversations rather than product and investor activities.

TechNext Asia built the full sales infrastructure and placed a full-time dedicated rep: 160 hours per month covering full pipeline ownership from prospecting to close to onboarding handoff, with a 90-day ramp plan, CRM setup, battle cards, objection guide, and weekly async standups with the founding team.

The Challenge
  • No documented sales process — every deal was handled differently based on the founder's intuition and relationship
  • No CRM — deals tracked in a personal Notion database that only the CEO could update
  • No onboarding kit for a new hire — product docs were scattered across Google Drive with no structure
  • CEO spending 40% of weekly time on sales conversations, blocking product and fundraising priorities
  • No churn monitoring process — customer departures were only discovered when a renewal invoice bounced
  • No battle cards or objection guide — rep would have no reference for competitive conversations
  • First 60 days of a new rep typically wasted on figuring out the product rather than generating pipeline
Our 3-Phase Approach
Month 1

Build the Foundation (Learn Phase)

  • Wrote FT rep job description — hired Amirul Haziq, 2.5 years B2B fintech sales, quota-attainment history verified
  • Built onboarding kit in Google Drive: product one-pager, ICP profile, competitive battle cards (3 competitors), objection guide (top 10 objections), email/call scripts
  • Set up Odoo CRM — 6 pipeline stages configured, custom fields for deal value, payment volume processed, and referral source
  • Ramp plan: Month 1 — 100% learning. No cold outreach in first 2 weeks. Product demos, ICP interviews, and script rehearsal only
Month 2

Pipeline Build (Assist Phase)

  • Amirul transitioned to assisted prospecting — CEO joined first 4 discovery calls as silent observer, then handed over independently
  • Built prospect list of 180 companies matching ICP — LinkedIn + Crunchbase research exported to Google Sheets
  • Launched cold email sequence (3-email) and LinkedIn outreach cadence — 20 contacts per day, tracked in Odoo CRM
  • Weekly async standup: Amirul posts voice note in group chat — what moved, what's stuck, what he needs — founder replies within 2 hours
Month 3

Full Ownership (Own Phase)

  • Amirul owned all 58 active pipeline deals independently — CEO formally stepped out of all sales conversations
  • Closed 4 deals in Month 3 — RM94,000 combined ARR; first quota hit exactly on target
  • Introduced client onboarding handoff SOP: signed deal → Amirul schedules 30-min onboarding call with product team within 24 hours
  • Churn monitoring added: monthly check-in call with all active clients scheduled in Odoo — 4.1% monthly churn held below 5% target
