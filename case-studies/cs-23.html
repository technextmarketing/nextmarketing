🏢 Client Overview

Curvio Analytics is a B2B SaaS startup building an AI-powered workforce analytics platform for HR directors at Malaysian companies with 200–2,000 employees. The platform analyses attrition risk, performance distribution, and salary benchmarking using anonymised internal HR data — priced on an annual subscription model starting at RM48,000/year.

The founders came from McKinsey and a major HR consulting firm. The product was technically strong, the market timing was right, and the founding team had a network of early adopters. But when enterprise procurement committees asked for external validation — "where can we read about Curvio?" — the answer was nowhere. Zero press coverage, zero third-party mentions, and a domain authority of 16.

The PR engagement was commissioned not primarily for traffic, but for credibility: enterprise B2B buyers in Malaysia expect to see media coverage before committing to a six-figure annual subscription. Press placements would serve as sales enablement as much as brand awareness.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • Zero press coverage history: no press releases ever published, no journalist relationships, no editorial mentions anywhere online — a blank slate for a company asking enterprise buyers to trust it with sensitive HR data
  • B2B SaaS PR is harder than consumer PR: fewer journalists cover enterprise software in Malaysia, news hooks must be business-relevant (not product features), and story angles require careful framing as business news rather than marketing
  • Founders with no PR experience: neither co-founder had written a press release, knew which publications to target, or had any journalist contacts — starting from ground zero
  • Domain authority of 16: company blog and website had minimal backlinks, meaning Google was not yet treating Curvio as an authoritative source — limiting organic credibility signals
  • No newsworthy event in the pipeline: no funding round, no product launch, no partnership to announce — the PR strategy would need to be built on thought leadership and data storytelling rather than hard news
  • Enterprise sales cycle concern: Curvio was in active pipeline conversations with 3 prospects who had explicitly said they wanted to "check the credibility of the company" before proceeding — the PR timeline needed to produce results within 90 days
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1
Press Release, Media List & HARO Launch
  • Identified primary news hook: Curvio had aggregated anonymised attrition rate data from 12 early-access clients — a proprietary dataset of Malaysian workforce trends that no other company had published. This became the press release angle: "Malaysian Employee Attrition Rose 23% in 2025 — New HR Analytics Data Reveals Which Industries Lost the Most Talent"
  • Wrote and edited press release in Claude AI + Google Docs: Headline / Dateline / Lead paragraph / 2 data-driven body paragraphs / CEO quote / Company boilerplate — reviewed by both co-founders for data accuracy
  • Built media contact list in Google Sheets: 28 journalists covering business, HR, technology, and startups across The Edge Malaysia, The Star Business, The Malaysian Reserve, Tech in Asia, DNA (Digital News Asia), KiniBiz, HR Asia, and 5 other publications
  • Signed up for HARO (Help a Reporter Out): received 3 relevant journalist queries in Week 2 — responded to all 3 within 1 hour with data-backed expert quotes from the CEO. Placed in HR Asia within 10 days (first placement)
Month 2
Personalised Pitch Campaign & Placements
  • Sent 20 personalised pitch emails using Claude AI: each tailored to the journalist's beat and recent articles — 3 subject line variants tested; best open rate: "Malaysian HR Directors Are Losing 1 in 4 Employees Every Year — We Counted" (43% open rate)
  • Followed up after 4 days with a 2-sentence nudge: "Following up on the attrition data story — happy to provide the full dataset and an interview with our co-founder if useful." 6 replies received
  • Secured 4 placements in Month 2: HR Asia (full feature), The Malaysian Reserve (data brief), Digital News Asia (startup profile), and KiniBiz (opinion piece by co-founder)
  • Co-founder opinion piece in KiniBiz: "Why Malaysian CEOs Should Fear the Attrition Cliff in 2026" — 3,400 organic reads in the first week. CEO shared on LinkedIn; 2 enterprise prospects commented directly on the post
Month 3
The Edge Coverage & Sales Impact
  • The Edge Malaysia replied to Month 1 pitch after a 6-week delay — requested a full interview and company background. Published a 680-word feature in The Edge Business section: "Analytics Startup Curvio Maps Malaysia's Hidden Attrition Crisis"
  • Within 48 hours of The Edge publication: 2 enterprise prospects in active pipeline responded unprompted with "We saw the article in The Edge — when can we get a demo?" Both converted to signed deals within 4 weeks
  • Total placements Month 3: 5 (The Edge Malaysia, The Star Business brief, 2 HARO quotes in international HR publications, 1 Malay-language business portal)
  • Domain authority improved from 16 to 28 over 3 months — driven by 9 publication backlinks including 2 high-DA sources (The Edge Malaysia DA 72, Digital News Asia DA 58)
  • Delivered PR performance report: all 9 placements with publication DA, estimated monthly readership, and backlink status; 20-journalist contact database for ongoing pitching; next-quarter PR calendar with 3 recommended story angles
