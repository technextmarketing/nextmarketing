Client Overview

PrimeLine Distribution is an industrial supplies distributor serving construction, manufacturing, and engineering companies across Peninsular Malaysia. With a 4-person sales team managing 200+ active accounts and a combined monthly revenue target of RM480,000, the company had been consistently hitting only 55–65% of quota for the preceding two quarters.

The General Manager was handling both operational and sales leadership responsibilities — a split focus that left the sales team without structured coaching, no pipeline visibility beyond a weekly check-in meeting, and no formal process for deal reviews or forecast accuracy. Two of the four reps had been with the company under two years and had never received formal sales coaching.

TechNext Asia provided a Fractional Sales Manager for 80 hours per month: running weekly 1:1 coaching sessions, building a KPI dashboard with pipeline health metrics, delivering monthly forecast review meetings, and creating a sales coaching playbook that the GM could use to continue the cadence independently after the engagement.

The Challenge
  • GM splitting time between operations and sales leadership — no consistent coaching structure for the team
  • 4-rep team with no formal pipeline review process — deals were discussed verbally with no CRM or tracker
  • Two junior reps had never received structured coaching — win rates of 18% and 22% vs 34% for senior reps
  • Monthly forecasts were gut-feel estimates — variance of ±35% making inventory planning and cash flow management difficult
  • No post-deal review process — reps weren't learning systematically from won or lost deals
  • No KPI dashboard — individual rep performance was assessed informally based on revenue only
  • Quota of RM480k/month being hit at 61% average — RM187k/month gap that was eroding margin and team confidence
Our 3-Phase Approach
Month 1

Audit, Baseline & Coaching Architecture

  • Pulled 3 months of pipeline and deal data from Google Sheets — calculated per-rep: leads, demos, proposals, closes, win rate, average deal size
  • Identified skill gaps by rep using Claude AI analysis — Rep 1: prospecting cadence, Rep 2: objection handling, Rep 3: negotiation, Rep 4: proposal quality
  • Built KPI dashboard in Google Sheets: pipeline value, calls, demos, proposals, closed deals, win rate, quota % — colour-coded green/amber/red
  • Set weekly 1:1 schedule: Tuesdays 30-min per rep, Fridays joint pipeline review — all in Google Calendar
Month 2

Coaching Execution & Playbook Build

  • Delivered 8 individual 1:1 sessions across all 4 reps — each session: review 2 real deals, identify one skill to practise, set one action before next session
  • Built sales coaching playbook in Google Docs: 5 sections — prospecting, discovery, demo, proposal, objection handling — 3 exercises per section
  • Ran first monthly forecast review: each rep presented top 5 deals, TechNext challenged assumptions, calculated weighted forecast
  • Month 2 team quota attainment: 74% — 13-point improvement from 61% baseline; forecast accuracy: ±12%
Month 3

Momentum & Handoff Preparation

  • All 4 reps hit personal quota targets in Month 3 — first time team collectively reached 87% of combined quota
  • Two junior reps improved win rates from 18–22% to 31–34% — primary driver: structured objection handling framework
  • Trained GM on coaching cadence: provided 12-week coaching calendar template, meeting agenda, and skill-tracking sheet
  • Delivered final forecast review presentation in Google Slides — team results vs quota, rep progress, forecast model accuracy, coaching continuity plan
