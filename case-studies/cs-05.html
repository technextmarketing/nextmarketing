Client Overview

CoreHive HR is a Malaysian SaaS company offering cloud-based HR, payroll, and attendance management software for SMEs. Their existing Google Ads account was spending RM8,000/month with an average ROAS of 1.8× and a cost-per-lead of RM210 — unsustainable for a bootstrapped SaaS with a RM450 average contract value.

The account had been built incrementally over two years without a coherent structure: broad match keywords with no negatives, ad groups mixing unrelated intent, and no conversion tracking linked to actual trials or demos. Budget was being absorbed by irrelevant clicks from job seekers searching for 'HR jobs' rather than HR software buyers.

TechNext Asia rebuilt the entire account from scratch — restructuring campaigns by buyer intent, rewriting all ad copy, implementing proper conversion tracking, and running a weekly bid optimisation cycle across a 90-day managed engagement.

The Challenge
  • Account running broad match keywords with no negative list — significant budget wasted on non-buyer queries
  • Zero conversion tracking — no visibility into which campaigns, ad groups, or keywords generated actual trials
  • Average CPA of RM210 against a RM450 ACV made unit economics unsustainable
  • Ad copy was generic ('Best HR Software Malaysia') with no differentiation or pain-point hooks
  • No audience segmentation — SME buyers, enterprise HR managers, and job seekers all pooled together
  • Landing page had no clear CTA above the fold and a 6-field form reducing trial sign-up rate
  • Competitor ads dominating 3 of the top 4 keyword clusters with stronger copy and extensions
Our 3-Phase Approach
Week 1–2

Account Rebuild & Tracking Setup

  • Linked Google Ads to GA4 — created 4 conversion events: Free Trial, Demo Request, Contact Form, Pricing Page View
  • Rebuilt campaign structure: 3 intent tiers — Brand, Competitor, Solution-Aware with separate budgets
  • Added 85-keyword negative list including job titles, HR department queries, and unrelated software terms
  • Switched all keywords to Phrase and Exact match — eliminated broad match entirely
Week 3–6

Ad Copy & Landing Page Optimisation

  • Wrote 3 RSA ad variants per ad group using Claude AI — tested angles: time savings, compliance, ease of use
  • Created dedicated landing pages per campaign with single CTA and 3-field form (name, email, company size)
  • Enabled all ad extensions: sitelinks (Pricing, Free Trial, Demo, Features), callouts, structured snippets
  • Set up dynamic remarketing to retarget users who visited pricing page but did not convert
Week 7–12

Bid Strategy & Scaling

  • Shifted winning ad groups to Target CPA bidding after 50+ conversions accumulated
  • Paused keywords with CTR below 2% after RM100 spend — reallocated budget to top 20% performers
  • Added 3 new ad groups targeting competitor brand names with alternative-framing copy
  • Weekly Search Term Report review — added 12 new negatives per week to tighten targeting
