Client Overview

HirePulse is a Malaysian B2B SaaS company offering AI-assisted talent acquisition software to HR leaders and in-house recruitment teams at mid-size and enterprise companies. Their primary lead generation relied on outbound email and referrals — LinkedIn Ads had been attempted once but paused after a RM310 cost-per-lead made it unviable.

The previous attempt failed due to a fundamental ICP mismatch: targeting had been set to broad job categories like 'HR' rather than precise seniority levels and decision-maker titles. Creatives were product screenshots with no value hook, and the landing page was the homepage rather than a dedicated lead capture experience.

TechNext Asia rebuilt the LinkedIn Ads programme from scratch — constructing a precise ICP audience by job title and seniority, producing Sponsored Content and InMail sequences, enabling Lead Gen Forms for frictionless capture, and running a 3-message InMail sequence targeting warm audiences who had engaged with content.

The Challenge
  • Previous CPL of RM310 made LinkedIn unit economics unworkable for a RM8,400 annual contract value
  • Audience targeting was broad HR job category — reached HR admin staff and not hiring managers or HR directors
  • Creative was a product UI screenshot with no outcome headline — zero emotional engagement
  • No Lead Gen Form — users were sent to homepage, producing a 94% drop-off rate from ad to lead
  • No InMail sequence — missing the highest-intent, lowest-competition LinkedIn ad format
  • Budget split evenly across all seniority levels — wasting spend on individual contributors with no buying power
  • No LinkedIn Insight Tag — zero remarketing capability and no conversion attribution
Our 3-Phase Approach
Week 1–2

ICP Targeting & Pixel Setup

  • Installed LinkedIn Insight Tag on website — enabled conversion tracking and retargeting audience build
  • Built precise ICP audience: HR Director / Head of Talent / People Operations Manager / CHRO — Seniority: Director+
  • Filtered by company size: 51–500 employees in Malaysia — excluded companies under 50 (too small) and 5,000+ (enterprise sales cycle)
  • Created Matched Audience from CRM contact list — used as exclusion (prevent re-targeting existing customers)
Week 3–6

Sponsored Content & Lead Gen Form

  • Produced 3 Sponsored Content ads: headline led with outcome — '14 days to first hire, not 90' — stat-forward creative
  • Enabled Lead Gen Form: 4 auto-fill fields (name, email, company, job title) — removed all friction from conversion
  • Ran A/B test: single image vs document ad (carousel PDF) — document ad outperformed by 34% on CPL
  • Set daily budget cap per campaign — capped learning phase burn at RM150/day until stable conversion rate established
Week 7–12

InMail Sequence & Retargeting

  • Built 3-message InMail sequence: Message 1 — insight on cost of slow hiring, Message 2 — HirePulse case study, Message 3 — 15-min demo ask
  • Retargeting campaign: LinkedIn company page visitors (60 days) → testimonial-led creative
  • Paused bottom 30% of job titles by CPL — concentrated budget on HR Director and Head of Talent
  • Month 3 CPL stabilised at RM94 — scaled budget 20% with ROAS maintaining above 4.2×
