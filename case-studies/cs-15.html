🏢 Client Overview

FastRoute Logistics operates a regional last-mile delivery network covering Klang Valley, Johor Bahru, and Penang. With a fleet of 140 vehicles and 3 depot locations, they spend RM12,000/month on marketing across Google Ads, Facebook Ads, LinkedIn, and SEO — managed by a 2-person in-house team.

The problem: each channel was tracked separately (or not at all), budget decisions were made on gut feel, and the marketing manager could not tell the CEO which channel was actually driving qualified leads. Monthly reporting was a manually compiled spreadsheet that took 3 days to build.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • 5 separate tools (Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, GA4, LinkedIn Analytics, Buffer) — no unified view
  • GA4 not configured correctly — goals not set, traffic sources mislabelled, conversion tracking missing
  • RM12,000/month budget split across 4 channels with no attribution model — no idea what worked
  • Monthly report took 3 days to compile manually — and still contained errors and data gaps
  • Facebook Ads: RM3,500/month with no conversion tracking — results unmeasurable
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1 · Jan
Setup — GA4 Fix, Tracking & Dashboard Build
  • Audited GA4: found 4 misconfigured goals, missing UTM parameters on all ad campaigns, direct traffic inflated by 40% due to missing source tagging
  • Fixed GA4: set up 6 conversion events (form submit, phone click, quote request, chat start, brochure download, newsletter signup)
  • Added UTM tracking to all Google Ads and Meta Ads URLs — traffic sources now correctly attributed in GA4
  • Built unified Google Sheets dashboard: auto-pulls data from GA4, manually updated with ads + social data → 2-hour monthly update vs. previous 3-day build
  • January report delivered: first clean month of data, channel-by-channel breakdown, CPL by source
Month 2 · Feb
Insight — 3 Underperforming Channels Identified
  • February data revealed: Facebook Ads (RM3,500/month) generating CPL of RM182 vs. Google Ads CPL of RM44 — 4× more expensive per lead
  • LinkedIn (RM1,800/month) generating 0 tracked conversions — Insight Tag misconfigured, leads going untracked
  • SEO organic: generating 38 qualified leads/month at near-zero marginal cost — massively undervalued in budget allocation
  • Recommendation: reduce Facebook Ads by 50%, fix LinkedIn tracking, increase SEO content budget
Month 3 · Mar
Reallocation — Budget Shift & ROI Improvement
  • RM1,750 moved from Facebook Ads to Google Ads (Exact Match campaigns) — CPL on Google dropped from RM44 to RM31
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag fixed — first trackable LinkedIn leads: 6 in March at CPL of RM120 (acceptable for B2B freight)
  • SEO blog content: 2 posts added targeting "logistics company Malaysia" and "same-day delivery Klang Valley" — organic leads up 28%
  • Overall marketing ROI (revenue attributed / spend): RM8.40 per RM1 spent in Mar vs. RM2.60 in Jan — +220% improvement
