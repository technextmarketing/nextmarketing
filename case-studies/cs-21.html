Client Overview

LifeCare Medical Group operates four aesthetic and general wellness clinics across Kuala Lumpur — Bangsar, Mont Kiara, Cheras, and Puchong. With 12 service categories and a marketing team of two, the group was struggling to maintain consistent content output, respond to online leads promptly, and track which marketing activities were driving actual clinic bookings.

Each location had its own WhatsApp line, Google Business Profile, and social media handle — managed inconsistently and with no central visibility. New enquiries arrived across 6 channels but were only followed up when a coordinator happened to check them, resulting in an average 8-hour response time and significant lead leakage.

TechNext Asia deployed the Full AI Marketing Suite: an AI content generation pipeline for consistent monthly output across all 4 locations, AI Ad Optimisation for their Google and Meta paid media, and an AI Chatbot on the group's main website that captured, qualified, and routed leads to the correct clinic automatically — all tied together with a centralised funnel dashboard.

The Challenge
  • Two-person marketing team managing 4 clinic locations with 12 service categories — content output was inconsistent and error-prone
  • Average 8-hour lead response time across 6 inbound channels — significant conversion loss to faster-responding competitors
  • No central lead tracking — enquiries from website, WhatsApp, social DMs, and Google tracked in separate spreadsheets with no unified view
  • Paid ads running without proper conversion tracking — spend decisions were based on impression data rather than actual bookings
  • No chatbot on the website — every FAQ and appointment inquiry required manual staff response
  • Content creation was reactive and ad-hoc — no monthly calendar, no brand consistency across locations
  • Google Business Profile reviews unmonitored — 3.8-star average from 14 unanswered negative reviews
Our 90-Day AI Suite Deployment
Phase 1

AI Content Pipeline (Weeks 1–3)

  • Built prompt library in Google Docs: 6 content types × 4 specialties — 24 master prompts covering all LifeCare services
  • Created monthly content calendar template in Google Sheets — 20 posts/month across 4 locations with clinic-specific customisation
  • Batch-produced first month's content in 4-hour Claude AI session: 20 social captions, 4 blog posts, 3 email campaigns
  • Scheduled all content via Buffer — first time all 4 locations published on the same day with consistent branding
Phase 2

AI Chatbot & Lead Capture (Weeks 4–6)

  • Deployed Tidio chatbot on main website — 12 FAQ flows covering top services: Botox, aesthetics, wellness packages, and booking
  • Chatbot lead capture: name, phone, preferred clinic location, and service interest — all auto-populated to Google Sheets
  • Integrated Sheets → Brevo: new chatbot lead enters 3-email welcome sequence + SMS within 5 minutes of capture
  • Average response time dropped from 8 hours to 4 minutes from chatbot deployment day
Phase 3

AI Ads & Unified Dashboard (Weeks 7–12)

  • Launched Performance Max for Google (clinic bookings) and Meta Advantage+ (aesthetic services) with AI bidding
  • Built master dashboard in Google Sheets: weekly data from Buffer, Brevo, GA4, Tidio, Google Ads, and Meta Ads in one view
  • Monthly AI strategy session: funnel data pasted into Claude AI — insights converted to Google Slides for management review
  • Month 3: 91% of new leads touched by automation before speaking to a human — team focused entirely on high-value conversions
