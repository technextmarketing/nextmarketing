🏢 Client Overview

AutoCare Premium Service Centre is one of Kepong's largest independent automotive workshops with 47 service bays, 22 technicians, and an average of 680 appointments booked per month. Services range from routine servicing and tyre replacement to engine overhauls and electrical diagnostics.

Despite a full appointment book, the service centre was losing significant revenue every month due to one persistent problem: customers booking and not showing up — with no reminder system in place and no consequence for cancellations.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • 30% no-show rate — 204 out of 680 monthly appointments resulted in empty bays and idle technicians
  • Average appointment value: RM108 — 204 no-shows = RM22,032 in monthly lost revenue
  • No reminder system in place — booking confirmation was a single WhatsApp message from the service advisor's personal phone
  • No re-engagement for lapsed customers — clients who hadn't returned in 6+ months received no communication
  • Promotional offers (tyre deals, servicing packages) communicated only via a physical banner at the workshop — zero digital reach
📋 Our Approach — Week by Week
Week 1–2
Setup — Brevo WhatsApp + Contact Import
  • Connected AutoCare's business WhatsApp number to Brevo — enabled WhatsApp Campaigns module
  • Imported 2,840 customer contacts from the service booking system (CSV export) into Brevo — segmented by: last visit date, service type, and vehicle type
  • Built opt-in confirmation: existing customers received a one-time "Would you like appointment reminders via WhatsApp?" message — 78% opt-in rate (2,215 opted in)
  • Created 3 message templates: (1) 48-hour reminder, (2) day-of reminder, (3) post-service follow-up
Week 3–4
Launch — Automated Reminders Go Live
  • Automated trigger: 48 hours before appointment → WhatsApp message: "Hi [Name], reminder: your [service] appointment is on [date] at [time]. Reply YES to confirm or RESCHEDULE to change."
  • Day-of reminder at 8am: "Good morning [Name]! Your AutoCare appointment is today at [time]. Our team is ready for your [vehicle plate]. See you soon!"
  • Week 3 results: no-show rate dropped from 30% to 11% in the first 2 weeks — 92% of messages opened within 5 minutes
  • 18 customers replied RESCHEDULE — bays refilled from waitlist the same day
Week 5–8
Scale — Promotions + Re-Engagement Broadcast
  • Launched monthly promotion broadcast: "Tyre rotation + alignment — RM88 this weekend only (was RM138). Book now: [link]" — sent to 2,215 opted-in contacts
  • Promo broadcast: 94% open rate, 12% click rate, 68 bookings attributed directly to message within 48 hours
  • Re-engagement campaign: 640 contacts with last visit 6+ months ago received: "We miss your car! Book a FREE 15-point inspection this month" — 89 bookings generated
  • Month 2 no-show rate: 4% — 27 no-shows vs. 204 before. Revenue recovered: RM19,116 + RM3,000 from re-engagement = RM22,116
