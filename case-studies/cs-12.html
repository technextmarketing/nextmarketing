🏢 Client Overview

Meridian Wealth Advisory is an independent financial advisory boutique in KL serving high-net-worth individuals with investment planning, insurance, and estate management. With 4 licensed advisors and a small admin team, they had been posting financial tips and market updates on LinkedIn and Instagram for 18 months.

The problem: every team member was creating their own posts using different design tools, font sizes, colour schemes, and photo styles. The result was a feed that looked inconsistent and unprofessional — undercutting the trust a financial services brand depends on.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • 4 advisors posting independently — 4 different design approaches, 3 different font families in use simultaneously
  • Stock photos from 3 different providers with inconsistent visual style (some professional, some casual)
  • No brand kit — logo file existed but was never applied consistently to social content
  • Engagement rate: 1.8% average — below financial services benchmark of 2.5%
  • Prospects told the firm: "Your posts don't look like a proper wealth management company"
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1 · Jan
Brand Audit & Visual Identity Rebuild
  • Conducted a full brand audit across 47 historical posts — identifying 6 different colour combinations and 3 inconsistent font families in use across 4 advisors' content, confirming the depth of the brand inconsistency problem.
  • Built a unified brand system and 3 master post templates (Insight Card, Market Update, and Client Tip) — locking colours, typography, and photo style to a single professional standard derived from the firm's existing logo and positioning.
  • Delivered Month 1's full 10-asset package — 6 social posts, 2 LinkedIn banners, and 2 email headers — all consistent for the first time in the firm's history, with engagement climbing from 1.8% to 2.5% immediately.
Month 2 · Feb
Consistency at Scale — All Channels
  • All 4 advisors briefed on brand kit usage — shared Canva team link so all could access templates without editing originals
  • Month 2 assets: 6 posts + 2 infographics ("5 signs you need an estate plan") + 2 ad creatives for Meta awareness campaign
  • Engagement rose to 3.1% average — 72% increase from 1.8% pre-engagement baseline
  • Infographic posts performed 3.2× better than text-only posts — adjusted Month 3 mix accordingly
Month 3 · Mar
Peak Results — 2.4× Engagement & Identity Locked
  • Month 3 assets: 6 posts + 2 infographics + 1 pitch deck cover + 1 capability one-pager
  • Engagement averaged 4.3% in March — 2.4× the pre-engagement baseline of 1.8%
  • One LinkedIn post ("3 mistakes Malaysian investors make with EPF") reached 4,200 impressions — best performing post in firm's history
  • Delivered full brand standards PDF — template library, do's & don'ts guide, Canva access instructions for ongoing self-service
