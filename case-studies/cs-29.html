Client Overview

AgileOps Technologies is a Malaysian SaaS company offering operations management and workflow automation tools to SMEs in the manufacturing, logistics, and retail sectors. With a strong inbound content strategy but no outbound sales function, the company's growth was entirely dependent on organic traffic and referrals — a ceiling that limited revenue scaling.

The founding team had attempted outbound before using a part-time virtual assistant for LinkedIn outreach, but the approach was untargeted: generic connection requests with no follow-up sequence produced 2–3 replies per month with almost zero conversion. What the company needed was a dedicated, trained SDR with structured daily activity targets, a well-defined ICP, and proper tooling.

TechNext Asia managed the full Junior SDR hire: writing the job description, shortlisting candidates, conducting a live role-play interview test, onboarding the hire with a 30-day ramp plan, and providing weekly coaching on script refinement, objection handling, and activity consistency — with a structured Google Sheets activity tracker reviewed every Friday.

The Challenge
  • Zero outbound function — 100% of revenue dependent on inbound and referrals with no predictability
  • Previous VA outreach attempt produced 2–3 replies per month — no ICP, no sequence, no follow-up cadence
  • No defined ICP for outbound: which company size, industry, and job title to target was undocumented
  • No email outreach infrastructure — no domain warming, no sequence tool, high risk of spam folder delivery
  • No onboarding plan for a new SDR — previous hires had been left to self-manage with no daily activity structure
  • Founders lacked time to coach a rep on an ongoing basis — needed a structured coaching framework
  • No distinction between a 'contact' and a 'qualified lead' — any reply was treated as a success
Our 3-Phase Approach
Month 1

Hire, Onboard & Build Infrastructure

  • Wrote SDR job description targeting 1–2 years experience, B2B outbound, SaaS or tech background preferred
  • Shortlisted 8 candidates from 34 applications — conducted live role-play test: cold-call the interviewer as a prospect
  • Selected hire: Hafizuddin Rahman — 1.5 years SDR experience at a KL IT distributor
  • Delivered 30-day onboarding plan: Week 1 product deep-dive, Week 2 ICP study + LinkedIn research, Week 3 script practice, Week 4 first 20 live outreach contacts with coaching review
Month 2

Ramp & Sequence Launch

  • Defined ICP: Operations Manager / Head of Operations / Supply Chain Manager — company size 50–500, manufacturing/logistics/retail
  • Built 200-contact prospect list using LinkedIn search — exported to Google Sheets with all data fields
  • Wrote 3-email cold sequence with Claude AI: Email 1 — pain point hook, Email 2 — case study proof, Email 3 — direct meeting ask
  • Daily activity targets set: 20 emails + 10 LinkedIn connects + 5 personalised follow-ups — tracked in daily standup Sheet
Month 3

Optimise & Scale

  • SDR hit 124 qualified leads in Month 3 — 3× Month 1 output as script confidence and ICP clarity improved
  • Added voice call cadence for warm email responders — 60-second discovery call qualifier before booking full demo
  • Weekly 1:1 coaching sessions: reviewed 2 real email replies, identified objection patterns, updated scripts accordingly
  • Introduced qualification gate: lead only passes to Senior Rep after confirming Budget + Authority + Need — 9 passed in Month 3
