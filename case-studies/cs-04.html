🏢 Client Overview

HireFlow Solutions is a pre-revenue HR technology startup building an AI-assisted applicant tracking system (ATS) for Malaysian SMEs. At the start of the engagement, the founding team of 6 was focused entirely on product development — with zero marketing infrastructure and no social media presence on any platform.

The founders understood that B2B SaaS requires consistent brand visibility before the sales cycle begins. They needed a multi-platform social presence established quickly to support an upcoming pitch to seed investors and to begin generating early market awareness.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • Zero social presence — accounts existed but were unclaimed or empty on all 4 platforms
  • No brand guidelines — only a logo file and a rough color palette shared in a Google Drive folder
  • B2B audience on LinkedIn required different tone and format from TikTok's short-video format
  • 16 posts/month needed across 4 platforms with a solo content operator (no agency team)
  • Founders unavailable for content review more than 30 minutes per week
📋 Our Approach — Week by Week
Week 1–2
Setup — Brand Identity & Platform Configuration
  • Built Canva Brand Kit from logo: extracted primary blue (#1D4ED8) + secondary navy + white, set Inter as system font
  • Created 4 platform templates: LinkedIn 1200×627, IG 1080×1080, FB 1200×630, TikTok cover 1080×1920
  • Optimised all 4 social media profiles — rebuilt bios, cover visuals, About sections, and website links with B2B-positioning copy aligned to HireFlow's ICP and investor-facing messaging
  • Built 60-day content calendar in Google Sheets: 16 posts/month = 4 per platform, alternating formats
Week 3–5
Publish — Platform-Specific Content Launch
  • LinkedIn (Tue/Thu 8am): long-form insight posts — "3 reasons ATS systems fail SMEs" style. Avg 180 impressions/post in Week 3
  • Instagram/Facebook (Tue/Thu 12pm): visual tip cards and product screenshots with benefits-led captions
  • TikTok (Wed/Fri 6pm): 30-second scripted videos — Hook: "Your HR team is wasting 4 hours a week on this..." — recorded by co-founder, edited in Canva Video
  • All posts scheduled through Buffer — zero missed publish dates across 60 days
Week 6–8
Optimise — Data-Driven Content Refinement
  • LinkedIn insight posts with specific statistics outperformed opinion posts by 2.3× in impressions
  • TikTok "problem-first" hooks (leading with a pain point) generated 4× more saves than product-led hooks
  • 3 LinkedIn posts triggered direct DMs from HR managers — converted to demo requests by founder follow-up
  • Delivered 60-day report: platform breakdown, top content formats, follower acquisition cost per platform
