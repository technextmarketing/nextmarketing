🏢 Client Overview

Nexova Consulting is a boutique business management consultancy in KLCC serving mid-market Malaysian companies across manufacturing, logistics, and professional services. The firm offers strategic planning, process improvement, and organisational change consulting — with 6 senior consultants and a 9-year track record of client retention.

Despite its strong reputation and consistent referral pipeline, Nexova had never invested in search engine optimisation. Their website — built in 2021 — had not been updated strategically since launch. Google Search Console had never been set up, meaning the team had no visibility into how (or whether) clients were finding them organically.

The trigger: a competitor half their size started appearing above them on Google for "strategy consultant Kuala Lumpur". That single discovery prompted the Managing Director to bring in NextMarketing for a structured SEO Starter engagement.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • Zero visibility in Google Search — 0 keywords tracked, no Search Console access, no awareness of organic performance
  • Website had 14 service pages with missing or duplicate meta descriptions — Google was auto-generating them from page copy
  • Blog section existed but had only 2 posts (published 2023) — neither targeted a specific keyword or had an SEO brief
  • 94% of monthly sessions came from direct or referral traffic — organic search contributed just 6% despite being the highest-intent channel
  • Mobile page speed score of 58/100 — 31 uncompressed images across service pages causing slow load times
  • 8 broken internal links and 3 redirect chains found in initial audit — undermining crawlability
  • Competitor outranking on 5 core service terms despite being a smaller firm with fewer years of experience
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1
Audit, Setup & Quick Wins
  • Installed Google Search Console — added HTML verification tag, verified domain property, submitted XML sitemap — 28 pages indexed within 72 hours
  • Ran full technical audit using Claude AI + manual review: 23 issues flagged — 14 missing meta descriptions, 8 broken internal links, 3 redirect chains, 31 uncompressed images
  • Used Claude AI to generate 48 target keywords grouped by intent — branded, service, location, and long-tail clusters. Loaded into Google Sheets tracker with columns: Keyword / Search Volume / Difficulty / Current Position / Target Page
  • Fixed all 8 broken internal links and resolved the 3 redirect chains within week 1 — quick win before any content work began
  • Compressed all 31 images via TinyPNG → mobile page speed improved from 58 to 81 within 10 days
Month 2
On-Page Optimisation & Internal Linking
  • Rewrote meta titles and descriptions across all 14 service pages — each under 58 and 155 characters respectively, keyword-matched to target term per page
  • Updated H1 tags on 9 pages where they were generic ("Our Services") to keyword-specific ("Business Process Improvement Consulting — Kuala Lumpur")
  • Built a full internal linking map in Google Sheets — every service page now links to at least 2 related pages; blog posts link back to their parent service page with target anchor text
  • Created 22 new internal links across the site without adding new content — just restructuring existing pages and blog posts to point to high-priority service URLs
  • Submitted all 14 updated pages to Search Console → Request Indexing — first keyword movements observed by end of week 6
Month 3
Content Launch & Monitoring
  • Published 4 keyword-targeted blog posts (avg 960 words each) written with Claude AI, edited for Nexova's consulting voice — each post directly targets a cluster keyword and links back to its parent service page
  • Post 1: "Business Process Improvement Checklist for Malaysian SMEs" → ranked Position 9 within 3 weeks
  • Post 2: "How to Choose a Management Consultant in Malaysia" → ranked Position 14 by end of month
  • Delivered 90-day SEO report: keyword movement table, top 5 traffic-driving pages, CTR by query, and 6 recommended next steps for Month 4–6
  • Set up automated monthly Search Console export to Google Sheets — Nexova team now has self-service visibility into keyword performance without needing a dashboard tool
