🏢 Client Overview

TechLine Distribution is a Subang Jaya-based IT hardware distributor representing 6 international brands including networking equipment, servers, and enterprise storage. Their team of 12 sales representatives serves over 200 active reseller and enterprise accounts across Klang Valley and Johor.

Despite strong product knowledge and established client relationships, TechLine was consistently losing deals to competitors in final proposal stages — not because of price, but because of presentation quality. Their materials were outdated Word documents assembled individually by each rep, with no consistent structure or brand identity.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • All 12 reps using personal Word/PowerPoint templates — no company standard, no brand consistency
  • Proposal acceptance rate: 22% — well below the IT distribution industry benchmark of 35%
  • Meeting-to-demo conversion: 28% — prospects frequently asking for "more professional documentation" before committing to a demo
  • Existing materials: 3-year-old product sheets with outdated pricing, no case studies, no visual hierarchy
  • 3 large enterprise deals lost in Q4 2025 — feedback cited "documentation quality" as a deciding factor
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1 · Feb
Foundation — Brand System + Core Collateral
  • Reviewed all existing materials — identified 3 most-used document types: (1) Product pitch deck, (2) Company capability statement, (3) Product one-pager
  • Built Canva Brand Kit for TechLine: corporate blue + white + dark grey, Montserrat Bold headings, Inter body text
  • Created master pitch deck template: 12 slides — cover / company overview / problem-solution / product lineup / 3 case study slides / pricing summary / next steps / contact
  • Rewrote all deck copy with value-led language, executive summary framing, and benefit-led product descriptions — transforming the pitch from a feature list into a buyer-outcome narrative
  • Produced 3 product-specific pitch decks (networking, servers, storage) — each customisable by rep in under 10 minutes
  • One-pager template built: 1-page A4 — product name, key specs, use case, 3 client logos, CTA
Month 2 · Mar
Scale — Capability Statement + Rep Training
  • Produced company capability statement: 4-page PDF — company history, brand portfolio, client list, service SLAs, contact directory
  • Built 6 product one-pagers (2 per product category) — reps can select and email directly after discovery calls
  • Trained all 12 reps via 45-minute Canva walkthrough session — how to customise templates, add client-specific content, export to PDF
  • Integrated with email workflow: one-pager templates exported to PDF sent via Brevo with open-rate tracking
  • Month 2 results: proposal acceptance rate rose from 22% to 32% — reps reporting that prospects commented on material quality
