🏢 Client Overview

TeaNature is a Malaysian premium organic tea brand producing hand-blended loose-leaf teas using ethically sourced Cameron Highlands and Sabah botanicals. Priced between RM38 and RM92 per tin, the brand targets urban professionals, wellness-focused consumers, and corporate gifting buyers. The brand launched its e-commerce store in January 2026 with zero social following, zero reviews, and zero brand awareness.

The founding team had invested heavily in product quality, packaging design, and an award-winning flavour range — but had no marketing budget for paid ads and no social media presence to build organic reach from. With a gifting season (Chinese New Year post-season and Hari Raya) approaching in March-April, TeaNature needed to build brand credibility and purchase intent quickly without the long runway that purely organic content requires.

The Influencer Outreach engagement was the primary go-to-market channel: identify and partner with the right micro-influencers in the wellness, lifestyle, and food-and-beverage niches whose audiences matched TeaNature's premium buyer profile — and convert their reach into brand awareness and first-time purchases.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • Zero brand awareness — TeaNature had 0 Instagram followers, 0 TikTok followers, and 0 public reviews at launch. No influencer would reference social proof that didn't exist yet
  • No influencer management experience: founding team had never run an outreach campaign, negotiated rates, briefed a content creator, or tracked influencer ROI
  • Premium positioning conflict: micro-influencers in the wellness space typically expect RM300–RM800 per post, which strained the initial RM8,000 campaign budget
  • No promo code or affiliate tracking system in place — making it impossible to attribute any sales to specific influencer posts without setting up a tracking infrastructure first
  • Saturated niche: "tea" content on Instagram Malaysia heavily skewed toward bubble tea and F&B brands — finding influencers whose audience actually matched organic loose-leaf tea buyers required careful vetting beyond follower count
  • Brand brief didn't exist — no influencer campaign guidelines, no do's and don'ts, no approved hashtags, no approved captions format. Everything had to be built from scratch before outreach began
  • Short timeline: 90 days to launch, generate sufficient UGC for brand credibility, and convert brand awareness into Hari Raya gifting purchases before the seasonal window closed
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1
Influencer Research, Vetting & Infrastructure
  • Used Claude AI to identify 12 content niches where organic wellness micro-influencers were most effective in Malaysia: lifestyle/morning routine, home aesthetics, mindful living, corporate gift reviews, skincare/health, and 7 others
  • Searched Instagram and TikTok using niche hashtags (#wellnessMalaysia, #slowliving, #morningroutine, #gifting) — vetted 60 accounts down to a shortlist of 30 based on: 10K–80K followers, engagement rate above 4%, authentic comments (no generic "Nice!" spam), and audience demographics matching TeaNature's buyer profile
  • Built Google Sheets tracker: Influencer Name / Handle / Platform / Followers / ER% / Email / DM Status / Rate Offered / Rate Agreed / Post Date / Promo Code / Units Sold / Revenue
  • Set up Shopify promo code system: each influencer given a unique 15% off discount code (TEANATURE_[NAME]) — all purchases tracked by code in Shopify dashboard
  • Sent 20 personalised outreach DMs using Claude AI templates — each mentioning a specific recent post the influencer had published; offered free product bundle (RM180 value) + 10% commission on tracked sales
Month 2
Partnership Activation & Content Brief Delivery
  • 14 influencers agreed to partner — 8 on commission-only basis (free product + 10% tracked sales), 6 accepted RM400 flat fee + free product. Total spend: RM2,400 cash + RM2,520 product at cost = RM4,920 total campaign cost
  • Wrote the TeaNature Influencer Campaign Brief using Claude AI: 2-page Google Doc covering brand story, 3 key messages ("ethically sourced", "hand-blended", "premium gifting"), content do's and don'ts, approved hashtags (#TeaNature #PremiumTea #GiftWithIntention), caption structure, and posting schedule
  • Briefed all 14 influencers via WhatsApp voice notes + the Google Doc brief — answered questions in 48 hours, approved creative concepts before posting
  • First posts published in Week 6: lifestyle influencer Ain Sofea (@ainsofea_) posted a "morning ritual" reel featuring TeaNature Jasmine Gold — 68,400 TikTok views in 72 hours, 214 promo code uses in the first 24 hours
  • Ran weekly check-in WhatsApp messages to all 14 partners: "How's the content coming along? Here's a draft caption if it helps [paste Claude AI draft]"
Month 3
Scaling Top Performers & Hari Raya Push
  • Identified top 5 performing influencers by promo code revenue: offered a second post (additional product bundle as compensation) timed for the Hari Raya gifting period in April 2026
  • Second wave of content focused specifically on gifting: "The best Hari Raya gift for someone who has everything" — 3 of the 5 posts each exceeded 100,000 TikTok views
  • Total campaign: 14 influencers × 1 post each (Month 2) + 5 influencers × 1 additional post (Month 3) = 19 total pieces of content published
  • Revenue directly attributed to influencer promo codes: RM84,000 across both months (Units sold: 2,210 tins). Campaign ROI: RM84,000 revenue ÷ RM9,980 total campaign cost (cash + product) = 8.4× ROI
  • TeaNature Instagram grew from 0 to 3,840 followers organically from influencer post tags; TikTok grew to 1,200 followers. Delivered 90-day influencer report with promo code breakdown, ROI per influencer, and top 5 performers for ongoing partnership
