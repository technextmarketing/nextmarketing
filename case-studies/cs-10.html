🏢 Client Overview

DataSync IT Solutions provides managed IT services, cloud migration, and cybersecurity solutions to Malaysian SMEs. Despite 8 years in business with a solid referral network, the company had never invested in digital content — their website had no blog and no organic search presence.

Competitors of similar size were publishing monthly blog posts and ranking for terms their buyers searched daily. DataSync was invisible in search despite having genuine expertise to share. The goal: build an SEO content foundation that compounds over time.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • Zero blog content — website had no articles, no internal linking structure, no organic pages
  • Organic sessions: 280/month — almost all branded searches ("DataSync IT"), zero discovery traffic
  • 0 keywords in Google's top 100 results for any non-branded term
  • Contact form averaging only 3 submissions/month — low web visibility = low inbound
  • No Google Search Console set up — no visibility into how the site performed in search
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1 · Jan
Foundation — Keyword Research & First 4 Posts
  • Set up Google Search Console — establishing the technical foundation for organic search tracking and identifying existing crawl issues before the first post was published.
  • Conducted buyer-intent keyword research for Malaysian IT services — identifying 12 high-priority, low-competition topics spanning awareness, consideration, and decision-stage search queries, each mapped to a dedicated blog post.
  • Published 4 blog posts in January — managed IT costs, cloud migration readiness, cybersecurity audits for SMEs, and on-premise vs. cloud comparisons — covering the full spectrum of DataSync's target buyer journey.
  • All 4 posts indexed within 24 hours of publication — by end of January, organic sessions had grown 11% and monthly contact form submissions increased from 3 to 4.
Month 2 · Feb
Optimise — Content Depth & Internal Linking
  • Search Console data after 30 days: Post 1 appearing in positions 18–24 for target keyword — optimised title tag and added 2 FAQ sections
  • Posts 5–8 published: server backup, IT outsourcing, remote work security, IT helpdesk comparison
  • Added internal links from new posts back to Month 1 posts — each post now receiving at least 2 internal links
  • Post 1 moved to position 11 after optimisation — one spot from Page 1
Month 3 · Mar
Results — Page 1 Rankings & Inbound Leads
  • Posts 9–12 published — included long-tail: "how much does IT support cost per user Malaysia", "best cloud storage for SME Malaysia"
  • 8 keywords confirmed on Page 1 via Search Console performance report (positions 1–10)
  • Organic sessions grew from 280 → 686 (month 3 vs. pre-engagement)
  • Contact form submissions: 3 → 8 per month — 2 of 8 directly mentioned reading a blog post
  • Delivered content tracker: 12 posts, their rankings at 30/60/90 days, traffic per post
