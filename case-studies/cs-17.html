Client Overview

InvoiceNow SaaS is a cloud-based invoicing and billing platform serving Malaysian SMEs and freelancers. The company had a growing free-trial user base but a poor conversion rate to paid plans — primarily because leads were treated uniformly regardless of engagement level, and no automation connected marketing activity to the sales team.

Their Brevo account contained 18,000 contacts but no segmentation: every contact received the same 4-email nurture sequence regardless of whether they'd opened one email or clicked through to pricing three times. Sales reps were manually reviewing Brevo contact profiles daily to identify hot leads — an inefficient process that introduced 72-hour average handoff delays.

TechNext Asia designed and built a full-funnel marketing automation architecture: a 10-signal lead scoring model, multi-step behavioural trigger workflows, an SMS + email multi-channel sequence for high-intent signals, and a Zapier-Odoo CRM bridge that auto-created tasks the moment a lead crossed the sales-ready threshold.

The Challenge
  • 18,000 contacts in Brevo with zero segmentation — hot leads and cold subscribers receiving identical communication
  • No lead scoring — sales team manually reviewing contact activity with 72-hour average handoff lag
  • Single 4-email nurture sequence applied to all contacts regardless of product engagement behaviour
  • No sales alert system — reps only discovered hot leads when they happened to check Brevo manually
  • CRM (Odoo) and marketing (Brevo) operating in complete isolation — no data sync between systems
  • Pricing page visits and demo requests not triggering any automated follow-up or rep notification
  • Email-only nurture with no SMS channel — missing the highest open-rate touchpoint for Malaysian audiences
Our 3-Phase Approach
Week 1–2

Segmentation & Lead Scoring Architecture

  • Audited all 18,000 contacts — created 4 core segments: Hot Leads, Trialists, Newsletter Only, Cold (no opens 90 days)
  • Built 10-signal lead scoring model in Brevo: email open +5pts, link click +10pts, pricing page +20pts, demo request +40pts, trial signup +30pts
  • Set three scoring thresholds: Cold (<20pts), Warm (20–59pts), Hot (60+pts) — mapped to rep action SLAs
  • Connected Brevo to Odoo CRM via Zapier: contact score ≥60 → auto-create Odoo task assigned to next available rep
Week 3–6

Multi-Channel Workflow Build

  • Built 5-email nurture sequence: split by segment — Trialist track vs Lead track with different content and CTAs
  • Created pricing-page trigger: contact visits pricing 2+ times within 7 days → immediate SMS + email within 15 minutes
  • Added abandoned trial workflow: trialists who haven't logged in for 7 days → 3-email win-back sequence with feature highlight
  • Set up sales alert email: rep receives instant notification when contact crosses Hot threshold — includes contact name, company, score, and last 5 actions
Week 7–12

Optimisation & Scaling

  • Analysed first 6 weeks of scoring data — recalibrated weights: trial login +15pts added (previously untracked event)
  • A/B tested nurture email subject lines on Warm segment — winner achieved 34% open rate vs 21% control
  • Expanded multi-channel: added LinkedIn retargeting trigger for Hot contacts — coordinated email + ads touchpoint
  • Month 3 results: MQL volume increased 122%, sales handoff time reduced from 72h to 38min
