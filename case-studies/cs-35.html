Client Overview

NetSphere IT Solutions is a managed IT services provider in Puchong offering server management, cybersecurity monitoring, helpdesk support, and infrastructure projects to SME and mid-market clients. With 34 active service contracts and an average deal value of RM28,000, the company was managing its entire quoting and invoicing process through manually-assembled Word documents, email-based approvals, and QuickBooks invoices sent as PDF attachments.

The finance director had flagged a DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) of 54 days — well above the company's 30-day payment terms. The root cause was a slow quote-to-invoice cycle: quotes took 2–3 days to produce (each from scratch), e-signature was done by printing, signing, scanning, and emailing back, and invoices were manually created only after the signed document was received — adding another 1–2 days to the cycle.

TechNext Asia implemented Odoo Sales with 3 quotation templates, enabled e-signature, configured auto-invoice generation on deal confirmation, and connected Stripe as a payment provider so clients could pay directly from the invoice link — eliminating every manual step between 'quote approved' and 'invoice issued'.

The Challenge
  • Quotes produced from scratch in Word documents for every deal — 2–3 day average production time with inconsistent quality
  • No e-signature workflow — approval process required printing, signing, scanning, and emailing signed copy back
  • Invoice only created manually after signed document received — adding 1–2 days to an already slow cycle
  • No payment link on invoices — clients received a PDF with bank details and had to manually initiate a bank transfer
  • DSO of 54 days against 30-day payment terms — RM340,000 in outstanding receivables at any given time
  • 3 service categories (retainer, project, ad-hoc) each had different pricing structures — no standardised templates
  • Finance team spent 8–10 hours per week on quoting, follow-up, and payment chasing
Our 3-Phase Approach
Week 1–2

Odoo Sales Configuration & Templates

  • Installed Odoo Sales module → Configuration → Quotation Templates → created 3 templates: Monthly IT Retainer (pre-filled services + SLA), Project-Based (milestone billing), Ad-Hoc Support (hourly rate schedule)
  • Wrote professional T&C with Claude AI: payment terms, service scope, cancellation clause, liability cap — approved by director and embedded in Odoo quotation footer
  • Built product catalogue in Odoo: 28 service line items with standard pricing — all available for 1-click addition to any template
  • Configured approval workflow: quotes over RM50,000 require Director approval before sending — Odoo internal approval gate
Week 3–4

E-Signature & Auto-Invoice

  • Enabled e-signature in Odoo: Settings → Sales → 'Signature on order' — digital signature box appears at bottom of every quote
  • Auto-invoice configured: Odoo → Sales → Invoicing Policy → 'Based on ordered quantities' — invoice generates automatically the moment client signs
  • Set payment terms per template: Retainer → 30-day net, Project → 50% upfront + 50% on delivery, Ad-Hoc → 14-day net
  • Integrated Stripe as payment provider — every Odoo invoice now includes a 'Pay Now' button linking to a Stripe-hosted checkout page
Week 5–12

Adoption & Finance Impact

  • Ran 2-hour team training — all 6 sales reps and 2 finance staff walked through template → quote → sign → invoice → pay cycle
  • Tested full flow with a real RM12,000 retainer renewal — quote sent, client e-signed in 18 minutes, invoice auto-generated, client paid via Stripe in same session
  • Week 8 data: average quote-to-invoice time reduced from 4.5 days to 5.5 hours — 94% reduction
  • Month 3 DSO: 38 days (down from 54) — RM128,000 reduction in outstanding receivables; target of 30 days on track
