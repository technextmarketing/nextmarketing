Client Overview

OmniCraft Engineering is a Klang Valley industrial equipment supplier and service provider, offering sales, installation, and maintenance of industrial automation components to manufacturing clients across Malaysia. With 6 sales reps, a 4-person finance team, and an operations team managing 200+ active service contracts, the company had grown to RM8M annual revenue while still running entirely on a combination of Excel, email, and WhatsApp.

The COO had mapped the problem precisely: a lead could sit in a sales rep's WhatsApp for 3 days without anyone else knowing about it. Once a deal was won, the handoff to finance for quoting and to operations for delivery scheduling required 4–6 separate email threads. Payment status was tracked in a personal Excel file maintained by one finance executive who was the single point of failure for all receivables visibility.

TechNext Asia led the complete Odoo Full Sales Pipeline Suite implementation: CRM, Sales (quoting + e-sign), Appointments (delivery scheduling), and Accounting — all configured individually and then connected into a single leads-to-cash workflow with proper user access controls, a full data migration from existing systems, and a 2-week parallel operation period before the old systems were officially switched off.

The Challenge
  • Entire pipeline managed across WhatsApp, email, and 7 different Excel files with no single source of truth
  • Won deals handed off to finance and ops via email threads — an average of 4–6 emails per deal before work began
  • No real-time payment status — finance executive's personal Excel was the only receivables tracker
  • Sales reps had no visibility into delivery status — clients calling reps for delivery updates instead of ops
  • No centralised customer database — the same client company existed under 4 different names across different files
  • Zero automation between systems — every stage transition required manual data re-entry by multiple people
  • Management reporting was a monthly Excel exercise that took 2 days to compile and was outdated by the time it was reviewed
Our Full Odoo Suite Implementation
Phase 1

Foundation — CRM & Pipeline (Weeks 1–2)

  • Mapped full business flow in Google Sheets: Lead → CRM Stage → Demo → Quote → E-Sign → Delivery → Invoice → Payment — identified the Odoo module responsible for each step
  • Installed and configured Odoo CRM first: 7 custom stages, email alias (sales@omnicraft.my), auto-assignment rules by rep territory, lead scoring model
  • Created unified customer database in Odoo Contacts — deduplicated 480 customer records from 7 Excel files into a single master list
  • Full data import: 94 active leads and 200+ service contracts migrated to Odoo CRM with verified stage and rep assignments
Phase 2

Sales, Appointments & Accounting (Weeks 3–6)

  • Configured Odoo Sales: 4 quotation templates (equipment sale, installation, service retainer, spare parts), e-signature enabled, auto-invoice on order confirmation
  • Set up Odoo Appointments for delivery scheduling: ops team creates time slots, sales rep books from won opportunity — customer auto-receives confirmation email
  • Connected Odoo Accounting: chart of accounts, payment terms (30-day retainer, 50/50 project), Stripe payment link on all invoices
  • Set user access: Sales team — CRM + Sales modules only; Finance — Accounting only; Ops — Appointments + delivery view; Admin — full access
Phase 3

Integration, Migration & Go-Live (Weeks 7–12)

  • Built cross-module automation: Won CRM opportunity → creates Sales Quote → signed quote → auto-creates Invoice → invoice paid → updates Accounting — zero manual re-entry
  • Ran 2-week parallel operation: team used both Odoo and old Excel/WhatsApp systems simultaneously — compared outputs daily for accuracy
  • Day 26: all pipeline data in Odoo matched Excel perfectly — old systems officially switched off; confirmed with COO
  • Odoo live reporting: COO can see full pipeline value, open invoices, delivery schedule, and rep performance in one dashboard — monthly report compilation reduced from 2 days to 20 minutes
