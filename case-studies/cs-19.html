Client Overview

TerraGear is a Malaysian outdoor and activewear brand offering performance running gear, trail shoes, and gym apparel through their Shopify store and Lazada storefront. Their paid media was managed manually — a marketing coordinator adjusting bids weekly based on intuition, resulting in inconsistent performance and significant budget waste during off-peak hours.

The brand had tried Google's Smart Bidding briefly in 2024 but abandoned it after two weeks when ROAS dipped during the learning phase — a common mistake that prevented the AI from completing its optimisation cycle. Meta Ads were running standard placements with manual bid caps, underutilising Meta's Advantage+ algorithm designed for exactly their product category.

TechNext Asia led the full transition to AI-managed paid media: enabling Google Performance Max with a properly structured asset group, launching Meta Advantage+ Shopping Campaign, establishing clear rules for the learning phase, and implementing a 14-day review cycle that scaled budgets without disrupting the AI's learning curve.

The Challenge
  • Previous Smart Bidding attempt abandoned after 12 days — AI never completed learning phase before being paused
  • Manual bid adjustments 3–4 times per week were constantly resetting Google's algorithm learning cycle
  • Meta campaigns running manual bid caps that prevented Meta AI from optimising across placements and audiences
  • Creative assets in both platforms were 6+ months old — Performance Max requires fresh assets to test effectively
  • No exclusion lists in Google — branded searches and competitor queries mixing with product intent campaigns
  • ROAS targets set unrealistically high (8×) on day 1 — forcing algorithm to restrict delivery to meet an impossible target
  • No systematic 14-day review cycle — budget changes were reactive to daily fluctuations rather than trend-based
Our 12-Week AI Transition Approach
Week 1–2

Asset Preparation & Account Configuration

  • Produced 5 new creative assets in Canva per platform: lifestyle imagery, product flat-lay, athlete-in-use, UGC-style video, brand story
  • Set realistic initial ROAS targets: Google 3.5× (vs previous manual 2.4×), Meta 4× — targets designed to allow learning phase completion
  • In Google Ads: disabled all Manual CPC campaigns — migrated budget to Performance Max with 15 assets loaded per asset group
  • In Meta: launched Advantage+ Shopping Campaign with RM150/day — strict instruction to zero adjustments for first 7 days
Week 3–6

Learning Phase Management

  • Monitored Performance Max daily but made zero bid or budget changes — allowed full 14-day learning cycle to complete
  • Google Performance Max exited learning phase on day 11 — ROAS climbed from 1.8× (learning) to 3.8× in week 3
  • Meta Advantage+ exited learning phase at day 9 — cost per purchase dropped 28% within 48 hours of exit
  • Added brand campaign separately in Google Search to protect branded queries — prevented PMax from over-bidding on brand traffic
Week 7–12

Scale & Optimisation Cycles

  • Increased Google PMax budget 20% on day 21 — ROAS held at 4.9× after 4-day stabilisation period
  • Increased Meta budget 20% on day 24 — CPP improved further to RM68 as lookalike modelling matured
  • Uploaded 3 new creative assets to PMax at week 8 — replaced 2 underperforming assets identified by asset reporting
  • Month 3: combined ROAS across both platforms reached 5.6× — 133% improvement vs pre-AI manual baseline of 2.4×
