🏢 Client Overview

PixelForge Digital is a boutique full-service digital marketing agency in Mont Kiara serving 14 active retainer clients across e-commerce, F&B, and professional services. The 8-person team includes 2 content writers, 2 designers, 2 account managers, and 2 technical staff.

Content production had become the agency's biggest bottleneck. Their two writers were responsible for blogs, social captions, ad copy, email newsletters, and website copy across all 14 clients — an impossible workload that had created a 3-week content backlog and was causing client dissatisfaction.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • 2 writers producing 6–8 content pieces/month combined — across 14 clients, that's less than 1 piece per client per month
  • 3-week backlog: clients requesting content on Week 1 received it in Week 4 or later
  • Average cost per piece (writer salary + overhead): RM850 — unsustainable at client retainer rates
  • Brand voice inconsistency across clients — rushing content led to generic, interchangeable copy
  • 2 client complaints received in December 2025 about missed content deadlines
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1 · Jan
Pipeline Design — AI Workflows Per Content Type
  • Documented 6 content types in the agency's output: blog posts, social captions (IG/LinkedIn), Google ad copy, email campaigns, product descriptions, website landing page copy
  • Built a Claude AI prompt template for each content type — including brand voice guidelines as context in each prompt
  • Created client-specific voice profiles in Google Docs: tone, vocabulary, topics to avoid, example approved content — loaded into each prompt as context
  • Month 1 output: 20 pieces across 8 clients — all delivered on time. Previous pace: 6–8 pieces/month
  • Cost per piece in Month 1: RM298 (same writer time, 3.5× more output)
Month 2 · Feb
Scale — 24 Pieces, Template Refinement
  • Writers shifted role: from writing from scratch → prompt engineering + editing AI output → higher leverage work
  • Built a content brief template in Google Sheets: client, content type, target keyword, CTA, tone, word count → feed directly into Claude AI prompt
  • 24 pieces delivered in February — backlog eliminated by Week 3
  • Quality check: account managers reviewed 10 random pieces — 9/10 rated "client-ready without major edits"
  • Cost per piece: RM260 — further reduced as prompts improved
Month 3 · Mar
Optimise — 28 Pieces, RM180/Piece, Brand Voice Locked
  • Established master prompt library in Google Docs: 6 content types × 14 client voice profiles = 84 ready-to-use prompts
  • 28 pieces delivered in March — agency's highest monthly output ever by 3×
  • Cost per piece: RM180 — 79% reduction from pre-AI baseline of RM850
  • Clients unaware of AI involvement — brand voice maintained across all accounts per account manager feedback
  • Agency added 2 new retainer clients in March — citing content capacity as their competitive advantage
