🏢 Client Overview

PixelForge Digital is a boutique full-service digital marketing agency in Mont Kiara serving 14 active retainer clients across e-commerce, F&B, and professional services. The 8-person team includes 2 content writers, 2 designers, 2 account managers, and 2 technical staff.

Content production had become the agency's biggest bottleneck. Their two writers were responsible for blogs, social captions, ad copy, email newsletters, and website copy across all 14 clients — an impossible workload that had created a 3-week content backlog and was causing client dissatisfaction.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • 2 writers producing 6–8 content pieces/month combined — across 14 clients, that's less than 1 piece per client per month
  • 3-week backlog: clients requesting content on Week 1 received it in Week 4 or later
  • Average cost per piece (writer salary + overhead): RM850 — unsustainable at client retainer rates
  • Brand voice inconsistency across clients — rushing content led to generic, interchangeable copy
  • 2 client complaints received in December 2025 about missed content deadlines
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1 · Jan
Pipeline Design — Structured Workflows Per Content Type
  • Mapped all 6 content types in the agency's output — blog posts, social captions, ad copy, email campaigns, product descriptions, and landing page copy — and built a structured production workflow for each, incorporating client-specific brand voice guidelines to ensure consistency across all 14 accounts.
  • Created individual voice profiles for each client in Google Docs — capturing tone, vocabulary, topics to avoid, and approved example content — so every piece produced matched the client's established brand voice without requiring full account manager review from scratch.
  • Month 1 output: 20 pieces across 8 clients, all delivered on time — 3.5× the previous monthly pace, at a cost per piece of RM298 using the same writer headcount.
Month 2 · Feb
Scale — 24 Pieces, Backlog Eliminated
  • Writers shifted roles from drafting from scratch to brief review and quality editing — a higher-leverage use of their time that increased throughput without adding headcount or extending working hours.
  • Built a standardised content brief template in Google Sheets capturing client, content type, target keyword, CTA, tone, and word count — enabling any brief to flow directly into the production workflow with no friction.
  • 24 pieces delivered in February — 3× the previous pace — eliminating the 3-week backlog by Week 3. Quality spot-check: 9 of 10 randomly reviewed pieces rated "client-ready without major edits." Cost per piece: RM260.
Month 3 · Mar
Optimise — 28 Pieces, RM180/Piece, Brand Voice Locked
  • Established a master content template library in Google Docs — 6 content types × 14 client voice profiles = 84 ready-to-use production templates — fully documented and transferable to the agency team for independent use going forward.
  • 28 pieces delivered in March — the agency's highest monthly output ever by 3×. Cost per piece reached RM180, a 79% reduction from the RM850 pre-engagement baseline.
  • Brand voice remained consistent across all 14 accounts with zero client complaints — account managers confirmed outputs matched expectations without major edits. The agency added 2 new retainer clients in March, citing expanded content capacity as a competitive advantage.
