🏢 Client Overview

FitHouse KL is a boutique fitness studio in Mont Kiara offering reformer Pilates, HIIT, and yoga classes with a maximum of 12 participants per session. At RM80–RM120 per class, the studio targets the premium segment — young professionals and expatriates in the Mont Kiara, Damansara, and Sri Hartamas corridor.

The studio had 840 Instagram followers and had never published a video. Content consisted of static Canva graphics posted 2–3 times per month, with an average of 12–18 likes per post and no measurable class bookings attributable to social media. The head trainer had a high-energy, camera-ready personality — a natural video asset that was going completely unused.

The Short-Form Video engagement set out to establish a consistent 4-video monthly publishing cadence across Instagram Reels and TikTok — scripted, recorded by the trainer, edited in CapCut, and optimised for the algorithm with proper captions, hooks, and hashtags.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • Zero video history — the head trainer had never recorded a Reel or TikTok and was anxious about being on camera despite being confident in a class environment
  • 840 Instagram followers, mostly existing clients — no mechanism for organic discovery or reaching potential new customers who had never heard of FitHouse
  • Studio class slots (12 per session) frequently had 3–5 empty spots per day despite warm leads in DMs who had inquired but never booked
  • Static graphics performing poorly: average reach of 180 per post, engagement rate of 1.8% — well below the 5%+ threshold for algorithm promotion
  • No knowledge of video editing tools: CapCut, Canva Video, captions, and audio layering were completely unfamiliar to the studio team
  • No publishing schedule: content was posted reactively when someone "had time" — sometimes 3 times a week, sometimes nothing for 2 weeks
  • TikTok account existed but had 0 posts and 0 followers — a blank starting point on the highest-reach short-video platform in Malaysia
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1
Script System, Recording Comfort & First 4 Videos
  • Built a 12-video script pipeline in Claude AI: 4 per month for 3 months — covering workout tips, "day in the life" content, myth-busting fitness claims, and class transformation hooks
  • Each script followed the proven format: 3-second hook ("You're doing planks wrong — here's why") → 5-second problem → 15-second solution (3 quick tips) → 7-second CTA ("Try a class — link in bio")
  • First recording session: trainer recorded 4 videos in 45 minutes using iPhone 13 Pro in portrait mode, ring light on, white studio wall as background — no extra equipment purchased
  • Edited all 4 in CapCut: imported footage → trimmed clips → added auto-captions (corrected 3 errors) → background music at 18% volume → exported at 1080p
  • Published on Instagram Reels and TikTok simultaneously — Video 1 (Pilates plank form correction) reached 4,200 views in 48 hours; TikTok version reached 8,900 views in 72 hours
Month 2
Momentum, Viral Video & Class Bookings
  • Trainer now fully comfortable on camera — recording sessions dropped from 45 minutes to 22 minutes for 4 videos as she found her on-camera rhythm
  • Added Canva Video branded intros and outros (3 seconds each) with FitHouse logo and amber accent colour — brought visual consistency to all videos
  • Video 6 ("3 signs your core is weaker than you think") went semi-viral: 28,400 views on TikTok, 9,100 on Instagram Reels — gained 820 followers in a single day
  • First direct class bookings attributed to video: 6 people DM'd referencing the viral video within 48 hours of posting; 4 converted to booked classes within the week
  • Responded to every comment within 1 hour of posting — algorithm reward was visible: engagement rate jumped from 1.8% (static posts) to 8.4% on video content
Month 3
Consistency, Community & Sold-Out Classes
  • Published 4 videos on consistent schedule: Monday and Thursday at 7pm — highest engagement window identified from Month 2 TikTok analytics
  • Introduced testimonial video format: existing client shot a 30-second "before I discovered reformer Pilates" script — 14,200 views, 62 comments, 3 new class bookings in 24 hours
  • TikTok account grew from 0 to 2,140 followers; Instagram grew from 840 to 2,060 — combined gain of 3,360 across both platforms
  • Group classes fully booked for 8 consecutive weeks by end of Month 3 — studio added a waitlist system for the first time in its 3-year history
  • Delivered 90-day video performance report: view rate by video type, follower growth breakdown, best-performing hooks, and Month 4 content calendar with 4 scripts ready to record
