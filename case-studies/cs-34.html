Client Overview

MindBridge Psychology Centre is a private psychology practice in Damansara offering individual therapy, couples counselling, and psychological assessments. With 5 therapists, each managing their own appointment schedules via a combination of WhatsApp, personal Google Calendar, and a paper diary at the reception desk, the clinic was experiencing a 24% no-show rate — each missed session representing a RM300 lost slot.

Appointment confirmations were sent manually via WhatsApp the day before — a process handled by a single receptionist who was also managing walk-ins, phone calls, and billing. When the receptionist was on leave, confirmation messages frequently weren't sent, and no-show rates spiked to over 35%. Double-booking was occurring 2–3 times per month as therapists updated their personal calendars without notifying reception.

TechNext Asia deployed Odoo Appointments with full Google Calendar synchronisation for all 5 therapists — creating appointment types per therapy category, building automated confirmation and reminder sequences, and distributing the booking link across all the clinic's touchpoints: email signatures, WhatsApp Business profile, and the website contact page.

The Challenge
  • No online booking system — all appointments booked via WhatsApp, requiring back-and-forth for availability
  • 24% no-show rate representing approximately RM21,600/month in lost revenue from empty therapy slots
  • Double-bookings occurring 2–3 times per month — therapists and reception maintaining separate calendars
  • Confirmation messages sent manually by one receptionist — process broke down entirely during staff leave
  • 5 therapists with 5 different personal calendar setups — no centralised availability view for reception
  • No reminder system — clients received one WhatsApp message the day before, nothing automated
  • Online booking was unavailable after office hours — clients who decided to book at 10pm had to wait until morning
Our Appointment System Implementation
Days 1–4

Odoo Setup & Google Calendar Sync

  • Installed Odoo Appointments module → created 4 appointment types: Individual Therapy (60 min), Couples Session (90 min), Psychological Assessment (120 min), 15-min Intro Call
  • Configured availability: Mon–Fri 9am–7pm, Saturday 9am–1pm — 15-min buffer between appointments, max 6 slots per therapist per day
  • Connected all 5 therapist Google Calendar accounts via Odoo OAuth integration — Odoo checks real-time availability before offering slots
  • Tested full sync: booked a test appointment in Odoo → confirmed it blocked in Google Calendar within 90 seconds
Days 5–14

Confirmation, Reminder & Distribution

  • Wrote booking confirmation email with Claude AI: friendly tone, includes appointment details, what to bring, Zoom/location info, cancel/reschedule link
  • Set up 48-hour reminder email and 2-hour SMS reminder: both automated via Odoo Appointments template system
  • Distributed booking link: added to Brevo email signature (all therapists), WhatsApp Business profile 'Book an Appointment' button, website contact page
  • Added booking widget embed code to website: clients can select therapist, appointment type, and time slot without calling or messaging
Week 3–8

Optimisation & No-Show Recovery

  • Added post-no-show automation: client who missed appointment → Brevo email within 1 hour with reschedule link and brief check-in message
  • Configured waitlist: fully booked slots show 'Join Waitlist' option — auto-notifies client when cancellation opens up their preferred therapist
  • Week 8 data: no-show rate dropped from 24% to 6% — 48h email reminder the single most impactful change (confirmed by client feedback form)
  • Online bookings now account for 64% of all new appointments — receptionist WhatsApp booking load reduced by 72%
