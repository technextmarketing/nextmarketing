Client Overview

DataBridge Analytics is a boutique data and business intelligence consulting firm based in KLCC, serving mid-market and enterprise clients across finance, logistics, and retail. With a team of 8 consultants and 3 account managers, the firm was closing RM1.2M in annual recurring contracts — but managing their entire sales pipeline across WhatsApp group chats, email threads, and a shared Google Sheet that was routinely out of date.

The lack of a structured pipeline meant deals were falling through the cracks: follow-ups were missed, proposal timelines were unclear, and management had no visibility into the status of active opportunities without scheduling a team call. Win rate was around 22% — acceptable but below what the team felt was achievable with better process visibility.

TechNext Asia designed and deployed a full sales funnel strategy: a buyer journey map, a 7-stage Odoo CRM pipeline, a BANT qualification framework, stage-specific email templates in Brevo, and Zapier automation that triggered follow-up communications the moment a deal moved between stages.

The Challenge
  • Entire pipeline managed in WhatsApp and a shared Google Sheet — no audit trail, version control, or stage visibility
  • No formal qualification criteria — reps varied widely in which leads they classified as 'qualified'
  • No stage-specific communication templates — proposal emails rewritten from scratch for every prospect
  • Management had zero real-time pipeline visibility — required manual check-ins with each rep to build forecasts
  • No automated follow-ups — reminders relied on reps' personal calendars and memory
  • Average sales cycle was 47 days with no clarity on which stage was causing the most deal stagnation
  • Win rate of 22% with no data on why deals were lost — no lost-deal tagging or analysis
Our 3-Phase Approach
Week 1–2

Buyer Journey Mapping & CRM Build

  • Workshop with sales team: mapped full buyer journey — 7 stages from First Contact to Won/Lost with exit criteria per stage
  • Configured Odoo CRM: replaced default stages — New Lead → Contacted → Qualified → Demo Booked → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Won/Lost
  • Created BANT qualification checklist: 6 yes/no criteria a rep must confirm before marking a lead Qualified — enforced as a required field
  • Imported all 84 existing deals from Google Sheets into Odoo CRM — mapped to correct stages, assigned to reps, added expected close dates
Week 3–6

Email Templates & Stage Automation

  • Wrote 7 stage-specific email templates with Claude AI — one per pipeline stage: initial outreach, qualification confirmation, demo follow-up, proposal cover note, negotiation check-in, won onboarding, lost re-engagement
  • Loaded all 7 templates into Brevo template library — reps click-to-send from Odoo opportunity card
  • Built Zapier automation: Odoo stage changes → trigger correct Brevo template to contact within 30 minutes
  • Set up lost-deal tagging: 5 loss reasons (price, timing, competitor, no budget, no decision) — mandatory field on lost deals
Week 7–12

Pipeline Review Cadence & Coaching

  • Weekly pipeline review introduced: every rep presents top 5 deals in Odoo — management reviews stage, expected close, and next action
  • Built pipeline velocity dashboard in Google Sheets: average days per stage, deal value per stage, rep win rate, loss reason breakdown
  • Identified bottleneck: 68% of deals stalling at 'Proposal Sent' — rewrote proposal email template with time-bounded CTA
  • Month 3 win rate: 28.2% vs 22% baseline — 64 new deals entered pipeline, 18 won in 90 days
