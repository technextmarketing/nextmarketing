🏢 Client Overview

Lumiere Beauty Co. is a Malaysian direct-to-consumer skincare brand selling 18 SKUs across its own website and Shopee. Founded in 2022, the brand grew its contact list to 22,000 subscribers through promotions and giveaways — but had never moved beyond a monthly newsletter sent to the entire list without segmentation.

The brand's email platform was Brevo, already set up but used only for basic broadcasts. There were no automations, no segmentation logic, and no revenue attribution — making it impossible to know whether email was contributing to sales at all. Open rates had declined from 18% to 8.4% over 6 months as audience fatigue set in from irrelevant mass sends.

The goal of the Email Marketing Pro engagement was to turn a dormant, declining list into a high-performing revenue channel — through behavioural segmentation, automated trigger flows, and a structured 8-campaign monthly cadence with A/B testing.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • 22,000 contacts treated as a single list — promotional emails sent to every contact regardless of purchase history, interest, or engagement level
  • Average open rate had dropped to 8.4% over 6 months — well below Brevo industry benchmarks of 25–30% for beauty e-commerce
  • No automated welcome sequence: new subscribers received their first email 3–5 weeks after sign-up when the next broadcast happened to go out
  • Abandoned cart behaviour triggered no follow-up — estimated RM28,000 in recoverable revenue going uncaptured monthly
  • Zero A/B testing history: every campaign used the same subject line format ("Lumiere Monthly Update — [Month]") with no variation
  • Email revenue completely untracked: no UTM parameters, no GA4 e-commerce integration — impossible to attribute revenue to any campaign
  • Unsubscribe rate climbing: 2.3% per send — 3× the industry average — indicating audience mismatch between content and recipient
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1
List Audit, Segmentation & Welcome Automation
  • Audited 22,000 contacts in Brevo: identified 8,400 with zero opens in 90 days, 6,200 customers (at least 1 purchase), 4,100 engaged non-buyers, 3,300 fresh opt-ins under 30 days
  • Created 4 master segments: Customers, Hot Leads (opened 3+ emails but never bought), Cold (90-day no-open), Newsletter Only — each receives only contextually relevant campaigns from this point forward
  • Suppressed the 8,400 cold contacts from all broadcasts for 60 days — open rate on first segmented send jumped from 8.4% to 26% immediately
  • Built a 3-email welcome sequence in Brevo Automations: Day 0 — personal welcome + brand story + 10% first-order code; Day 3 — bestseller showcase; Day 7 — skincare tips blog post + soft CTA
  • Welcome sequence achieved 58% open rate on Email 1 within first 2 weeks of activation
Month 2
Revenue Triggers & A/B Testing Programme
  • Built abandoned cart recovery flow: trigger = added to cart but no purchase after 2 hours → Email 1 (reminder, no discount) → wait 24h → Email 2 (5% off, 48-hour expiry); recovered RM14,200 in Month 2
  • Set up GA4 e-commerce integration and UTM parameters on every email link — first time Lumiere could see email-to-revenue data in real time
  • Launched A/B subject line testing programme: 2 campaigns per month tested with 5 subject line angles each — curiosity, urgency, benefit-led, personalised, and social proof
  • Best-performing subject line in Month 2: "Your skin is telling you something — are you listening?" (curiosity angle) → 44% open rate vs 19% for product-name variant
  • Sent 8 campaigns across 4 segments: 2 promotional (revenue), 3 educational (nurture), 2 new arrivals (engagement), 1 re-engagement (cold segment with win-back offer)
Month 3
Revenue Scaling & List Growth
  • Post-purchase follow-up sequence activated: 7 days after purchase → email requesting review → 14 days → cross-sell recommendation based on product purchased — generated RM8,400 in incremental repeat revenue
  • List growth accelerated: added embedded double opt-in form to website checkout page and blog sidebar → 3,520 new subscribers in Month 3 alone (+16% list growth)
  • Total email-attributed revenue for Month 3: RM22,800 — tracked via GA4 UTM parameters and Brevo conversion dashboard
  • Cold re-engagement campaign sent to suppressed 8,400 contacts: "We miss you" with 15% comeback offer → 14% reactivation rate, 1,176 contacts returned to active segments
  • Delivered 90-day email marketing report: segment performance, A/B test winners, revenue by campaign type, and Month 4–6 automation roadmap
