🏢 Client Overview

Vaultify Fintech is a B2B SaaS company providing a digital lending origination platform to licensed money lenders, cooperatives, and microfinance institutions in Malaysia. Their platform handles loan application, credit scoring integration, disbursement, and repayment tracking — serving 38 clients across 6 states.

Despite having a live website and a subject-matter expertise that competitors lacked, Vaultify had no content strategy. Their blog had 3 posts — all published in 2023, none targeting specific keywords, and all generating combined organic traffic of 47 sessions per month. Competitors without a superior product were ranking ahead of them for search terms their buyers used daily.

The Content Writing Pro engagement set out to build a pillar-and-cluster content architecture from scratch: one authoritative pillar page supported by targeted blog posts per month, all connected through internal links and Search Console tracking.

⚠️ The Challenge
  • Zero content strategy: 3 blog posts published since 2023, none with keyword targeting or internal linking — a blank slate with no SEO foundation
  • Organic blog traffic of only 47 monthly sessions despite the product solving a well-searched problem ("digital lending software malaysia", "loan management system")
  • No topic cluster or pillar architecture — isolated posts with no internal linking, no anchor text strategy, and no authority signal to Google
  • Competitors ranking on Page 1 for 12 of Vaultify's 20 target keywords — including a competitor with an objectively inferior product and older technology
  • Founders busy closing enterprise deals — no internal capacity to plan, research, write, or publish content consistently
  • Compliance-sensitive industry: every piece of content required accuracy checks against BNM guidelines before publication — adding a review step that slowed output without a clear workflow
  • No Google Search Console configured — no baseline data on impressions, clicks, or average position for any keyword
📋 Our Approach — Month by Month
Month 1
Pillar Strategy & Cluster Architecture
  • Ran full Search Console setup and baseline audit: 0 keywords in Top 50 for any target commercial term; only branded searches showing any impressions
  • Used Claude AI to generate a 6-month content pillar strategy: identified 3 topic clusters — (1) Digital Lending Technology, (2) Loan Portfolio Management, (3) Regulatory Compliance & BNM — each with 1 pillar page and 8 supporting blog posts
  • Mapped all 27 planned URLs into a Google Sheets content calendar: Pillar / Supporting Post / Target Keyword / Monthly Volume / Difficulty / Planned Month / Status
  • Wrote and published Pillar Page 1: "Digital Lending Software in Malaysia: The Complete Guide for Licensed Lenders" — 2,850 words, 8 internal anchor links, 4 external authority citations
  • Pillar page indexed within 48 hours; entered Page 2 (Position 18) for "digital lending software malaysia" within 3 weeks
Month 2
8-Post Publication Sprint & Internal Linking
  • Published 8 supporting blog posts (avg 1,050 words each) targeting long-tail keywords within Cluster 1: "loan origination system malaysia", "automated credit scoring SME lending", "digital disbursement system cooperative" and 5 others
  • Every post included: 2 internal links back to the pillar page using exact match anchor text; 1 link to a related supporting post; 1 external link to a BNM or government source for compliance credibility
  • Built full internal linking map in Google Sheets: every URL tracked with what it links TO and what links back TO it — ensuring no orphan pages
  • Post 4 ("How Licensed Money Lenders Can Reduce Loan Default Rates With Technology") entered Page 1 by end of Month 2, Position 7
  • Total blog organic sessions increased from 47 to 312 by end of Month 2 — +564% vs baseline
Month 3
Cluster 2 Launch & Content Refreshes
  • Launched Cluster 2: "Loan Portfolio Management" — pillar page 2 published (2,640 words); 8 supporting posts published covering portfolio risk, NPL tracking, collection automation, and Shariah-compliant lending workflows
  • Refreshed 3 Month 2 posts that had entered positions 11–15 using Claude AI update prompts: added 2025 BNM statistics, 2 new tips per post, and updated internal links — all 3 moved into Top 10 within 2 weeks
  • Set up automated monthly Search Console export to Google Sheets: Vaultify team now receives a weekly auto-populated keyword movement sheet without needing to log into any tool manually
  • Month 3 organic sessions: 192 (baseline) → 597 from blog alone; total site organic traffic: 47 → 193 → 597 across 3 months = +1,170% cumulative growth from blog content
  • Delivered 90-day report: keyword ranking table for all 27 tracked URLs, top 5 posts by traffic, internal link audit, and Cluster 3 (Compliance) content roadmap for Month 4–6
